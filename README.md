`main.py` copies `entry.png` - one image summing up the world news of Thursday 26 November 2026 - to /output/entry.png.

The day in one picture: Ukraine's strike on the Savasleyka airbase and Samara refineries, judges urging key Netanyahu charges be dropped, the second Houthi hit on Riyadh's PIF Tower and rising oil, COP31 ending with no climate deal, US-India tariff brinkmanship, the first Pakistani astronaut aboard China's Tiangong station, Asia's tech selloff, the Australia-Solomon Islands pact, Egypt's tanker fire, and Gaza.

Run: `python3 main.py`
Local test: `ENTRY_OUTPUT=./output/entry.png python3 main.py`
